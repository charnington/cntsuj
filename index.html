Welcome to my webpage! I'm a final-year undergraduate mathematics student at the University of Hong Kong.

My research interests lie in the ways analysis can be used to explore other areas of mathematics, particularly number theory, probability, and metric geometry. More recently, I've also become interested in how AI is shaping the mathematical community.

I enjoy teaching and explaining mathematics. Beyond the subject itself, I believe that learning to tackle mathematical problems helps us develop analytical and logical thinking skills that are valuable well beyond the classroom.

Please feel free to reach out if you're interested in collaborating: chanatip [at] connect.hku.hk.

I share Federico Ardila-Mantilla’s axioms.

A little more personal

Miscellaneous.

A small collection of questions and answers, mostly about life outside research. I'll add more to this page over time.

01

Where am I from?

I was born and raised in Nakhon Sawan, Thailand, where I spent the first 15 years of my life. I was then fortunate enough to be admitted to Kamnoetvidya Science Academy, where my passion for mathematics really began to grow. In 2023, I decided to study abroad at HKU—one of the biggest decisions of my life! I've also spent a total of eight months in Canada, first as an exchange student at McGill University and later as a summer research intern at the University of Manitoba.

02

How did I become interested in mathematics?

Well, the answer might sound cliché: it was simply the subject I was good at in school. I remember competing with my friends to see who could do mental arithmetic the fastest. Then, in high school, I started exploring more serious mathematics, like calculus and linear algebra. One question kept coming back to me: Why are the theorems we use actually true? That curiosity eventually led me to pursue a mathematics degree at university.

03

What are my hobbies?

I like watching horror movies (although, ironically, I'm quite easily scared by ghosts), listening to musics, and singing. I also enjoy hiking, trying new food, and spending time with cats and dogs.
